Terms of Service

App name: TikTok Bot (Content Posting)

What this app does

Posts videos to TikTok on your behalf via the official TikTok Content Posting API. It uploads a video file you provide and publishes it to your TikTok account with the caption you choose.

Data accessed

  • Your TikTok profile (display name, avatar)
  • Video upload and publish permissions
  • Your access token (stored only on your machine, not on a server)

What this app does NOT do

  • Read your DMs
  • Access followers, likes, or other users' data
  • Post without your explicit trigger
  • Share data with third parties

Revoking access

You can revoke this app's permissions at any time in your TikTok account settings under Connected Apps.
